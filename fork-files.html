The Fork Files

Every fork score from the Doughboys wiki, split by who gave it. The short version: Mitch is a touch softer than Nick, and guests are the toughest crowd at the table. And when a meal breaks the scale, it's almost always Mitch doing the breaking.

01 · The spread

Everyone lives at 3.5 to 4 forks

Share of each person's scores that land on each half-fork. Guests use the bottom of the scale more often than either host.

02 · Agreement

The hosts tie 40% of the time

Each square counts episodes with that pair of scores. The outlined diagonal is an exact tie; Mitch scored higher in 149 episodes, Nick in 123. Hover a square to see which episodes.

03 · The gap

When they split, it's usually by half a fork

Nick's score minus Mitch's on the same episode. The average gap is 0.07 forks in Mitch's favour. A paired Wilcoxon test puts that at p = 0.018: probably a real lean, but a small one.

04 · Over the years

2025 was a hard year for chain food

Average score per calendar year. Mitch warmed up after 2017 and has sat above Nick most years since; 2025 dragged everyone down together. 2026 is a partial year.

05 · Live shows

Put Mitch in front of a crowd and the forks come out

Average scores for studio episodes against the 48 live episodes. Everyone rates higher live, and Mitch jumps the most.

06 · Feuds

The biggest host splits

The top of this list has a story: at Steak 'n Shake 10, Mitch liked the food, called it "secretly 5 forks," and gave it 1 fork anyway to keep it out of the Platinum Plate Club.

07 · The guest book

Who's tougher than the hosts?

Guests with three or more scored appearances, ranked by how their score compares with the hosts' average on the same episodes. Left of zero means harsher than Nick and Mitch. Across all guest episodes, guests averaged 0.14 forks below the hosts (p = 0.001).

08 · Off the scale

The 6-fork club is mostly Mitch

Five forks is meant to be the top, but a handful of meals broke through it. Mitch gave 5 of the 8 scores above 5; Nick has done it once, at Guisados, alongside Mitch. Nicole Byer's 10 forks for Regina Pizzeria is the record.

09 · The floor

The worst meals on record

55 episodes got 5 forks or more from both hosts. At the other end, these are the lowest host averages in the data.

Nick hands out 1 fork or less on 4.6% of his reviews, twice as often as Mitch (2.4%).

Source: episode pages on the Doughboys fandom wiki (doughboys.fandom.com), scraped September 2026. Included: 456 main-feed episodes where both Nick and Mitch gave a single score. Scores above 5 forks are kept as given (the histograms group them as 6+). Left out: tournament and head-to-head episodes with several scores per host, pages with no fork table, and the grocery-store episodes scored in shopping carts, which run on a different scale. Themed units like spoons or "Morts" are counted as forks. When there are two guests, the guest score is their average.
